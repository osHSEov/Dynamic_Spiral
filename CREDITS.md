# Sources and asset credits

## Text sources (all from the supplied research notes, `source-materials/Spiral Dynamics.pdf`)

1. **Clare W. Graves**, "Levels of Existence: An Open System Theory of Values" (1970).
   The title and year come from the notes. The journal details shown on the site (*Journal of Humanistic Psychology* 10(2)) were added from general bibliographic knowledge. They are **not verified**, so check them before publishing. The site paraphrases the excerpt in the notes and does not quote Graves directly.
2. **Steve Dinan (Esalen Institute)**, summary of Don Beck and Christopher Cowan, *Spiral Dynamics: Mastering Values, Leadership, and Change*. No URL or date was supplied. Dinan's population and "power" percentages are deliberately not reproduced. His lists of example people, groups and countries are also left out.
3. **Sergey Litti / Litti Pro**, "Spiral Dynamics. Teal Organizations. Change Management" (video), known here only through the notes' summary table and the eight slides. No URL, date or timestamps were supplied.

Some short framing points come from general knowledge rather than the notes: Frederic Laloux as the source of the term "Teal organizations", and the note that Graves himself did not use colors. They are mentioned only to keep the frameworks distinct.
